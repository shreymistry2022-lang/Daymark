Account and Data Deletion

At Daymark, we respect your right to privacy and total ownership of your personal data. You have the complete right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal information at any time.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

You can instantly and permanently erase your account and all local and cloud data directly from within the Daymark mobile application:

1 Open the Daymark app on your device.

2 Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.

3 Scroll down to the bottom and tap Delete Account & Data.

4 Confirm the confirmation prompt by tapping Delete Forever.

This immediately deletes your authentication record, all synchronized habits, routines, goals, journal reflections, and resets your local app storage.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can submit a deletion request via email. Our team will verify your account email and delete all cloud data within 14 calendar days.

Submit Deletion Request

Please email us from the email address registered with your Daymark account with the subject: "Data Deletion Request".

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Account Credentials: User ID, email address, password hash, and OAuth authentication tokens.
  • Habits & Logs: All active and archived habits, past completions, measurable log metrics, and streak history.
  • Routines & Goals: All routine sequences, targets, milestones, and goal definitions.
  • Journal Entries: All personal reflections and habit-linked notes.
  • Preferences: Cloud-synced theme, reminder, and dashboard settings.

Data Retention Exceptions

We do not retain any of your personal tracking data following account deletion. Anonymized transaction receipts processed through Google Play Billing are retained only as required by Google Play accounting, tax, or legal regulatory obligations.
