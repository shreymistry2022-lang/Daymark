Complete Feature Manual

Everything you can do in Daymark

An overview of all modules built into Daymark to help you track habits, sequence routines, build consistency, and protect your privacy.

01 • HABIT TRACKING

Versatile Habit Types & Quit Habits

Support for simple Yes/No check-ins, numeric count targets (+/- increments), and measurable progress bars with custom units (e.g. 2000 ml water, 30 mins reading). Also includes Quit-habit tracking with lapse logging and 1-tap undo protection.

02 • SCHEDULING ENGINE

Schedule-Aware Streaks

Configure habits for daily, weekdays only, weekends, specific days of the week, or weekly targets. Streaks only count on days habits are scheduled — so a weekday workout won't penalize your streak on Sunday.

03 • DAILY DASHBOARD

Swiss Progress & Celebration

Clean high-contrast today overview featuring timezone-accurate greetings, a 7-day interactive week strip, category filters, and an animated paper-spread celebration when all daily habits are completed.

04 • ROUTINES BUILDER

Structured Morning & Evening Flows

Chain separate habits into automated routines. Start a morning or evening sequence to step through your rituals in order with a dedicated completion bar and scheduled routine reminders.

05 • GOALS & TARGETS

Link Habits to Life Milestones

Set overarching long-term targets (e.g. Read 50 books or Run 500 km) and automatically aggregate progress from linked daily habits. Set incremental milestones and celebrate major life wins.

06 • CALENDAR & HEATMAPS

Yearly Grids & Consistency Rates

View your consistency with a dynamic yearly GitHub-style intensity heatmap, monthly calendars, and 7-day / 30-day / 90-day rolling consistency rates. Easily inspect or log historical days.

07 • PRIVATE REFLECTIONS

Habit-Linked Journal

Record notes and reflections up to 10,000 characters per entry. Link thoughts directly to individual habits to identify triggers and patterns, or write standalone daily logs with full-text search.

08 • REMINDERS & WIDGETS

Smart Alerts & Home Screen Glance

Reliable local device notifications scheduled at your chosen times with automatic pause and archive cancellation. Includes a native Today home screen widget for iOS and Android.

09 • DATA PRIVACY & EXPORT

Secure Authentication & One-Tap Export

Creating a free account protects your habit history, synchronizes check-ins safely via Supabase, and allows one-tap full CSV/JSON data export anytime.
