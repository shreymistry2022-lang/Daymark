Daymark Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 | Last Updated: November 7, 2026

1. Local-First & Privacy-By-Design Philosophy

Daymark is built with a local-first architecture:

  • Your habits, routines, goals, journal entries, achievements, and app preferences are stored directly on your device.
  • You can use Daymark completely offline as a guest without creating an account or transmitting personal tracking data.
  • If you choose to create an account or sign in, your data is securely synchronized across your devices using our backend infrastructure.

2. Information We Collect

A. Data You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and authentication credentials. If you authenticate using Google or Apple Sign-In, we receive authentication tokens and your verified email address provided by the identity provider.
  • Habit and Productivity Data: Habits, schedules, frequency settings, check-ins, custom notes, routines, milestones, and personal journal reflections you enter.
  • App Preferences: Selected theme, accent color, week start day, notification preferences, date formatting, and timezone settings.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Device & Operating Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique app instance identifiers required for error tracking and notification routing.
  • Subscription & Purchase State: When purchasing Daymark Pro, purchase receipts and transaction identifiers are processed via Google Play Billing / Apple App Store and RevenueCat. We never collect, receive, or store your credit card numbers or financial details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for providing and improving the core service:

  • Delivering habit tracking, routine sequencing, milestone calculation, and local reminders.
  • Synchronizing your data across devices when you are logged in.
  • Verifying your Daymark Pro entitlement status.
  • Assisting you with customer support and bug resolution.

We never sell your personal data or share your personal tracking details with third-party advertisers.

5. Security & Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • All data transfers are encrypted in transit using modern TLS 1.3/HTTPS.
  • Sensitive device credentials and tokens are secured via hardware-backed SecureStore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Cloud databases enforce strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies guaranteeing that only you can access or modify your records.

6. Data Retention and Deletion (Your Rights)

  • Exporting Data: Export your complete data anytime as CSV or JSON from the Profile menu.
  • Deleting Account: Instantly wipe your account, cloud data, and local cache via Profile → Delete Account & Data.
  • Manual Request: Email us at support@daymark.app or smsm44186@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" to have all server records permanently expunged within 14 days.
